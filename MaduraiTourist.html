Thoonga Nagaram - The City That Never Sleeps

About Madurai

Madurai is one of the oldest living cities in India and the cultural heart of Tamil Nadu. With over 2500 years of history, it is famous for its towering temples, rich traditions, and delicious food.

Known as "Thoonga Nagaram", the city never truly sleeps. From the grand Meenakshi Temple to the streets filled with the aroma of Jigarthanda, Madurai offers a perfect mix of spirituality, history, and culture.

Famous Places to Visit

1. Meenakshi Amman Temple

2500+ year old temple with 14 colorful Gopurams. The heart of Madurai. Don't miss the evening pooja and sound & light show.

2. Thirumalai Nayakar Mahal

1636 la kattina royal palace. Famous for huge pillars and Indo-Saracenic architecture. Evening sound & light show is a must.

3. Gandhi Memorial Museum

South India's only Gandhi museum in a palace. Has the blood-stained cloth and exhibits on India's freedom struggle.

4. Alagar Kovil

20km from Madurai. Beautiful hill temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Peaceful and surrounded by nature.

5. Mariamman Teppakulam

Huge temple pond. Famous for the Float Festival in January. Great place for evening walk and photography.

6. Vaigai River

The lifeline of Madurai. Evening walk along the river bridge near Gandhi Museum is very popular among locals.

7. Kutladampatti Falls

35km from Madurai. 90ft high waterfall in green forest. Perfect for picnic and natural bath. Best in Monsoon season.

Distance: 35km from Madurai | Entry: ₹20

8. Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple

Arupadai Veedu la 1st. 1200 year old rock-cut cave temple. Murugan married Deivayanai here. Famous for weddings and Panguni Uthiram festival.

Distance: 8km from Madurai | Special: Hill temple + Holy tank

9. Pandi Kovil

Mattuthavani thandi, Meenakshi Mission Hospital kitta NH44 highway la iruku. Vehicle pooja and Sunday prayers ku romba famous. Madurai people oda kula deivam.

Distance: 10km from Mattuthavani | Landmark: Near Meenakshi Hospital, Othakadai
