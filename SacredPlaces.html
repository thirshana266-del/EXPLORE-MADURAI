Sacred Places of Madurai 🛕

Where Every Month is a Celebration

⛪ Famous Temples in Madurai

Spiritual + Festival Calendar

Madurai temples are famous not just for architecture, but for grand festivals that happen throughout the year.

1. Meenakshi Amman Temple

The Meenakshi Amman Temple is the soul of Madurai and one of the most famous temples in India. Dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi, an avatar of Parvati, and Lord Sundareswarar, an avatar of Shiva, this 2000-year-old temple is known for its 14 towering gopurams covered with over 33,000 colorful sculptures. The main highlights include the Hall of 1000 Pillars, the Golden Lotus Tank, and the evening "Palliarai" ceremony where Lord Shiva is taken to Goddess Meenakshi's chamber. The temple is not just a place of worship but a living center of art, culture, and tradition that attracts millions of devotees every year.

🎉 Major Festivals & Time:

Meenakshi Thirukalyanam - Chithirai Festival: April - 10 days. The biggest festival. Celestial wedding of Meenakshi & Sundareswarar.
Float Festival - Thai Theppam: January
Navaratri: September - October, 9 days
Best Time to Visit: April for Chithirai Festival
Address: Meenakshi Amman Temple, West Veli Street, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001
Timings: 5:00 AM - 12:30 PM, 4:00 PM - 9:30 PM

2. Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple

Thiruparankundram is one of the six sacred abodes of Lord Murugan. It is believed that Lord Murugan married Deivanai here. This 6th-century rock-cut temple is built inside a hill and is unique because it houses shrines for Murugan, Shiva, Vishnu, Vinayagar, and Durga. The temple architecture, natural caves, and the view of Madurai city from the top make it a significant pilgrimage and tourist spot. It is also famous for its healing water spring.

🎉 Major Festivals & Time:

Thaipusam: January - February. Kavadi and grand procession.
Panguni Uthiram: March - April. Marriage festival of Murugan.
Karthigai Deepam: November - December. Lamps on the hill.
Best Time to Visit: January for Thaipusam
Address: Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple, Thiruparankundram, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625005
Timings: 5:30 AM - 12:30 PM, 4:00 PM - 8:30 PM

3. Alagar Kovil - Kallalagar Temple

Located 21km from Madurai at the foothills of Alagar Hills, this temple is dedicated to Lord Vishnu as Kallalagar. It is famous for the grand Chithirai Festival in April, where Lord Kallalagar travels to Madurai to attend the celestial wedding of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar. The temple has beautiful Dravidian architecture, a golden vimana, and the sacred "Noopura Gangai" waterfall inside the temple complex. The peaceful surroundings and lush greenery add to its spiritual charm.

🎉 Major Festivals & Time:

Chithirai Festival - Kallalagar Entry: April. Kallalagar enters Vaigai River. Huge crowd.
Garuda Sevai: December
Best Time to Visit: April 12-15 for Kallalagar Vaigai Aruil Ezhuntharulum
Address: Alagar Kovil, Alagarkoil, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625301
Timings: 6:00 AM - 1:00 PM, 3:00 PM - 8:00 PM

4. Vandiyur Mariamman Temple & Teppakulam

The Vandiyur Mariamman Temple is dedicated to Goddess Mariamman, worshipped for protection from diseases. Right in front of the temple is the massive Vandiyur Mariamman Teppakulam, one of the largest temple tanks in Asia. Every year in January, the famous float festival is celebrated here with beautifully decorated deities on boats. The huge tank and the temple together create a breathtaking sight, especially during evenings.

🎉 Major Festivals & Time:

Teppam Festival - Float Festival: January. 5 days. Deities taken on decorated floats.
Aadi Festival: July - August. Special poojas for Mariamman.
Best Time to Visit: January for the night float festival
Address: Vandiyur Mariamman Temple, Vandiyur, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625012
Timings: 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM

5. Koodal Azhagar Perumal Temple

Koodal Azhagar Temple is one of the 108 Divya Desams dedicated to Lord Vishnu, located in the heart of Madurai city. The unique feature of this temple is that Lord Vishnu blesses devotees in three different postures: Standing on ground floor, Sitting on first floor, and Reclining on second floor. This 1000-year-old temple is believed to fulfill wishes related to wealth and clearing debts. The tall Rajagopuram and intricate pillars showcase classic Dravidian architecture. It is just 1.5km from Meenakshi Temple, so you can easily visit both together.

🎉 Major Festivals & Time:

Vaikunta Ekadasi: December - January. Vaikunta Dwaram opens.
Garuda Sevai: December. Lord Azhagar on Garuda Vahanam procession.
Chithirai Brahmotsavam: April. 10 day grand celebration.
Best Time to Visit: December - January
Address: Koodal Azhagar Perumal Temple, South Masi Street, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001
Timings: 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 4:30 PM - 8:30 PM
Distance from Meenakshi Temple: 1.5 KM

6. Pazhamudircholai Murugan Temple

Pazhamudircholai is the sixth and final abode of Lord Murugan among the Arupadai Veedu. Located 20km from Madurai inside the Alagar Hills forest, this temple is famous for its serene atmosphere and natural beauty. The name means "forest with falling fruits". It is the place where Lord Murugan tested Saint Avvaiyar with the famous "Sudaatha Pazham" story. The temple is surrounded by lush greenery, small waterfalls, and monkeys. Devotees believe praying here brings wisdom, clears education obstacles, and gives peace of mind. The journey through the forest itself is a spiritual experience.

🎉 Major Festivals & Time:

Thaipusam: January - February. Kavadi procession from Alagar Kovil.
Panguni Uthiram: March - April. Special abhishekam and poojas.
Karthigai Deepam: November - December. Entire hill is lit with lamps.
Best Time to Visit: Morning hours. Avoid afternoon due to forest heat
Address: Pazhamudircholai Murugan Temple, Pazhamudircholai Hills, Alagarkoil, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625301
Timings: 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM
Distance from Madurai: 20 KM. Bus: Route 44 from Periyar Bus Stand

7. Sri Pandi Kovil

Pandi Kovil is a historic Murugan temple located at the foothills of Thiruparankundram. Built and patronized by Pandya kings, hence called "Pandi Kovil". It is believed that Pandya kings prayed here before going to war. The temple is known for its powerful vibrations and quick blessings. This is one of the important temples in Madurai for devotees seeking victory in legal matters and protection. The architecture reflects Pandya style with strong stone structures.

🎉 Major Festivals & Time:

Thaipusam: January - February. Grand Kavadi and procession.
Panguni Uthiram: March - April. Marriage festival of Murugan.
Best Time to Visit: Tuesday and Friday mornings
Address: Pandi Kovil, Thiruparankundram Hill Base, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625005
Timings: 5:30 AM - 12:30 PM, 4:00 PM - 8:30 PM

8. Sri Immaiyilum Nanmai Tharuvar Temple

Located near Periyar Bus Stand in the heart of Madurai city, this is a very powerful and ancient Shiva temple. "Immaiyilum Nammai Kakkum Annai" means "The Mother who protects us in this life and the next". Devotees believe that worshipping here will bring protection in both this birth and the next birth. The main deities are **Lord Kasi Viswanathar** and Goddess **Visalakshi Amman**. It is believed that praying here gives the same blessings as visiting Kasi Viswanathar Temple in Varanasi. Hence it is also called "Madurai Kasi". People facing court cases, debts, and health problems come here to pray and believe their problems will be resolved. The temple is small but very peaceful. It is just a 2-minute walk from Periyar Bus Stand.

🎉 Major Festivals & Timings:

Pradosham: Twice every month. Evening 4:30 PM - 6:00 PM. Very famous here.
Maha Shivaratri: February - March. All-night pooja.
Karthigai Deepam: November - December.
Aadi Amavasai: July - August. Rituals for ancestors.
Best Time to Visit: Pradosham days and Monday mornings
Address: Sri Kasi Viswanathar Temple, East Gate, Near Periyar Bus Stand, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001
Timings: 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 5:00 PM - 8:30 PM
Landmark: 200 meters from Periyar Bus Stand East Gate

9. Sri Kasi Viswanathar Temple - Samanar Malai

Sri Kasi Viswanathar Temple is located on the hilltop of Samanar Malai, near Keelakuyilkudi, about 12 KM from Madurai. This is a 300+ year old Shiva temple. The main deities are Lord Kasi Viswanathar and Goddess Visalakshi Amman. Devotees believe that worshipping here gives the same blessings as visiting Varanasi Kasi. Hence it is also called "Thenn Kasi" and "Vaan Kasi" because it is situated on a hill. The temple is small but very peaceful. From the top, you can get a beautiful panoramic view of Madurai city, fields, and hills. Special abhishekams are performed during Pradosham and Maha Shivaratri. Since Jain beds and the Shiva temple are located together, this place is a perfect blend of history and spirituality.

🎉 Major Festivals & Timings:

Maha Shivaratri: February - March. All-night pooja and bhajans.
Pradosham: Twice every month. Evening 4:30 PM - 6:00 PM. Vilva pooja is very famous.
Karthigai Deepam: November - December. Grand lighting of lamp on the hilltop.
Tai Amavasai: January - February. Rituals for ancestors.
Best Time to Visit: Early morning or evening. The sunset view from the temple is amazing
Address: Sri Kasi Viswanathar Temple, Samanar Malai Hill Top, Keelakuyilkudi, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625018
Timings: 6:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM
How to Reach: 12 KM from Madurai. Take Route 19 bus to Keelakuyilkudi. Then climb approx 200 steps to reach the temple
Note: No vehicle access to the top. Only walking

⛪ Famous Churches in Madurai




10. St. Mary's Cathedral - East Gate

One of the oldest and most famous Catholic churches in Madurai. Established in 1841 by Jesuit missionaries. Located near East Gate, close to Meenakshi Temple. The architecture is beautiful with tall spires and stained glass windows. It is the main cathedral of the Madurai Roman Catholic Archdiocese. Peaceful atmosphere and daily masses are held here. Christmas and Easter celebrations are grand.

🎉 Major Festivals & Timings:

Christmas: December 25. Midnight mass and grand decoration.
Easter: March - April. Special services for 1 week.
Feast of St. Mary: September 8.
Mass Timings: Weekdays 6:00 AM, 7:00 AM. Sunday 6:00 AM, 7:30 AM, 5:30 PM
Address: St. Mary's Cathedral, East Gate, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001
Timings: 5:30 AM - 8:00 PM
Landmark: 1 KM from Meenakshi Temple, Near East Gate

11. Our Lady of Lourdes Church - K.K. Nagar

Known as "Madurai Lourdes". Famous for healing prayers and Thursday novenas. Has a large grotto of Mother Mary. Very peaceful.

🎉 Major Festivals & Timings:

Thursday Novena: Every Thursday. Special healing prayers.
Feast of Our Lady of Lourdes: February 11.
Address: Our Lady of Lourdes Church, K.K. Nagar, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625020
Timings: 5:30 AM - 8:00 PM

🕌 Famous Mosques in Madurai




12. Kazimar Big Mosque - Kazimar Street

One of the oldest and most famous mosques in South India. Built in 13th century by Hazrat Kazi Syed Tajuddin. Located in the heart of Madurai city near West Gate. Beautiful architecture with large prayer hall. Friday prayers have huge gatherings. Very peaceful and historic place.

🎉 Major Events & Timings:

Ramzan: April - May. Special prayers and community feast.
Bakrid: June - July.
Milad-un-Nabi: September - October.
Prayer Timings: 5 times daily. Jummah: Friday 12:30 PM
Address: Kazimar Big Mosque, Kazimar Street, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001
Timings: 4:00 AM - 9:00 PM
Landmark: Near West Gate, 1.5 KM from Meenakshi Temple

13. Goripalayam Dargah & Mosque

Famous Dargah and Mosque complex located near Goripalayam Bus Stand. Tomb of Hazrat Meer Ahmad Ibrahim and Meer Amir Ali. People of all religions visit here for blessings. Known for "Santhana Koodu" festival. Very crowded during Thursdays.

🎉 Major Events & Timings:

Santhana Koodu Festival: February. 10-day grand Urs.
Thursday Night: Special prayers and offerings.
Ramzan & Bakrid: Grand celebrations.
Prayer Timings: 5 times daily
Address: Goripalayam Dargah, Goripalayam, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625002
Timings: Open 24 hours for Dargah. Mosque: 4:00 AM - 10:00 PM
